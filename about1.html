About GarinFarm

GarinFarm is located in Barangay Huna, San Joaquin, Iloilo, Philippines. It was established to promote agricultural tourism in the province. The farm spans 14 hectares and is divided into different sections including the farm animals, the plantation, and the famous "Journey to Heaven" monument.

A Unique Three-in-One Destination

What sets GarinFarm apart is its unique identity as an integrated site that seamlessly combines agriculture, leisure, and spirituality. Owned by the Garin family, the property was developed to offer visitors a space for both recreation and reflection. It functions as a working farm where guests can observe agricultural practices, a resort with various outdoor activities, and a pilgrimage site that draws visitors seeking a moment of peace and contemplation.

The Journey to Heaven

The centerpiece of GarinFarm is the Pilgrimage Hill, often referred to as the "Stairway to Heaven" or "A Journey from Creation to Ascension". Visitors embark on a climb of approximately 480 steps, a path that is lined with life-sized dioramas and statues depicting significant biblical events, from the story of Creation and Noah's Ark to the Life, Passion, and Resurrection of Jesus Christ.

The climb culminates at the foot of a towering Divine Mercy Cross. To complete the experience, visitors pass through a dark Meditation Tunnel—symbolizing earthly struggles—before emerging into a blindingly white, ethereal space that depicts Heaven, complete with statues of angels and saints. This dramatic transition is designed to evoke a sense of spiritual renewal and awe.

Recreation and Leisure

Beyond its spiritual draws, GarinFarm offers a variety of activities for families and adventure seekers. The expansive grounds include a hilltop swimming pool, a large lagoon for boating and kayaking, and a 300-meter zipline that offers a thrilling ride over the water. Visitors can also enjoy horseback riding, fishing, or simply exploring the lush gardens and observing the farm animals, which include goats, ducks, and rabbits.

Visitor Information

  • Location: Barangay Huna, San Joaquin, Iloilo, Philippines.
  • Entrance Fee: Approximately PHP 150 per person.
  • Activities: Some activities have separate fees (e.g., Zipline, Swimming Pool, Boating).
  • Best Time to Visit: The site is popular during Holy Week for pilgrimage, but it is open year-round. Early mornings are recommended to avoid the heat and crowds during the climb.
  • Tips: Bring an umbrella, water, and wear comfortable shoes, as the climb can be steep and hot.
